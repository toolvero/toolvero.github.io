Paste a PEM PKCS#10 Certificate Signing Request. Decoding happens in your browser.

CSR details will appear here.

Why decode a CSR?

A CSR contains the identity and public key submitted to a certificate authority. This tool helps verify the requested common name and other attributes before a certificate is issued.
