Quickly translate CIDR prefixes into subnet and wildcard masks and inspect the binary representation of an IPv4 address.

What is CIDR notation?

CIDR notation combines an IP address with a prefix length such as /24. The prefix indicates how many bits identify the network. Converting CIDR into familiar subnet information helps administrators understand address boundaries and plan networks.

Common uses

  • Translate CIDR prefixes into subnet masks.
  • Inspect network boundaries from an IPv4 CIDR.
  • Use alongside the subnet calculator when planning address space.
