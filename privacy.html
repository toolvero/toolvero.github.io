Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This page explains how Toolvero handles information when you use the site.

Tool inputs

Most Toolvero utilities process input locally in your browser. Toolvero does not intentionally collect the text, configuration values, certificates, hashes or other values you enter into local-processing tools.

Important: Avoid entering production passwords, private keys, access tokens or other sensitive secrets into any online tool, including Toolvero.

Analytics

Toolvero uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate site usage, such as page views, approximate location, device/browser information and how visitors navigate the site. This helps identify useful tools and improve the site.

External services

Some tools may depend on external services. For example, the DNS Lookup tool sends the requested domain and record type to Google's public DNS-over-HTTPS service. Some pages also load third-party JavaScript libraries required for local parsing or conversion.

Cookies and local storage

Analytics may use browser storage or cookies according to Google's configuration and applicable rules. Toolvero also uses local storage to remember interface preferences such as light or dark mode.

Third-party policies

Services loaded from third parties are governed by their own privacy practices. Toolvero does not control those external services.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Toolvero adds features, analytics or monetization. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
