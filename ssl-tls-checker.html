SSL Certificate Expiry Checker

Paste a PEM X.509 certificate to check validity and days remaining locally in your browser.

Certificate status will appear here.

Why paste the certificate?

A static browser page cannot perform arbitrary remote TLS handshakes. Pasting a PEM certificate keeps the check reliable and local. Use Toolvero’s Certificate Decoder when you need full X.509 extension details.

Why check certificate expiration?

TLS certificates have a defined validity period. Expired certificates can break secure connections and cause outages, while certificates nearing expiration need renewal planning. This tool reads a pasted PEM certificate rather than attempting a cross-origin live connection to arbitrary servers.

Common uses

  • Check not-before and not-after dates from PEM data.
  • Confirm how many days remain before expiration.
  • Use with the certificate decoder for additional X.509 details.
